Individual-tree coordinates locate surveyed crowns/tops, not surveyed trunk bases.
Forest density, species mixture, wind motion and generated tree heights are
authored visual rules, not a local botanical inventory. Roots follow the game's
rendered terrain. Terrain smoothing, tree-age differences and modeled access
clearances can change the appearance relative to source surveys and imagery.
